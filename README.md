## 🌟 Key Highlights & Architecture

- **Zero-Dependency Setup**: Runs directly in any modern browser without needing Node.js, bundlers, or local server installations.
- **Deep Slate Glassmorphic Design System**: Engineered with dark mode aesthetics (`#07090E`, `#0C1017`), luminous accents (`#6366F1`, `#10B981`, `#F59E0B`), and custom Google Fonts (*Outfit* & *Plus Jakarta Sans*).
- **Comprehensive Simulated B2B Database**: Pre-populated with 20+ diverse, realistic decision-maker profiles across SaaS, AI, Fintech, E-Commerce, Cybersecurity, HealthTech, and Agencies.
- **Live LocalStorage Persistence**: Automatically retains saved pipeline prospects, custom additions, and workflow progression across page refreshes.

---

## ⚡ Core Features

### 1. Multi-Dimensional Lead Discovery & Filter Engine
- **Global Search**: Instant search across prospect name, job title, company name, domain, city, or tech stack (e.g. *Stripe*, *Salesforce*, *Kubernetes*).
- **Quick Preset Signals**:
  - `All Prospects`
  - `🔥 High Intent / Funding`: Filters to Series A-C funding events or expansion triggers.
  - `🚀 SaaS & AI Founders`: Focuses on C-level leaders in SaaS and Artificial Intelligence.
  - `🛍️ E-Commerce CMOs`: Curated marketing leaders in high-growth online retail.
  - `📈 Fast Hiring Spree`: Companies undergoing rapid headcount growth.
  - `📞 Direct Phone + 98%+`: Filters to records with verified direct phone lines and peak deliverability.
- **Faceted Sidebar Filters**: Real-time count badges for Industry Sectors, Buying Intent Triggers, Seniority, Company Sizes, and Geographic Regions.

### 2. Dual View Experience (Visual Grid & High-Density Table)
- **Visual Card Grid**: Displays prospect avatar, match score badge (`98% 🔥 Ultra`), company strip, detected tech stack, deliverability badge, and action triggers.
- **Data Table View**: Dense spreadsheet layout for rapid scanning with sortable headers and bulk selection.
- **Bulk Operations**: Multi-select leads to **Export to CSV**, save to pipeline, or generate batch outreach.

### 3. Slide-Over Prospect Intelligence Dossier
- In-depth profile breakdown with verified direct work email, corporate phone line, company headcount, estimated annual revenue, detected tech stack tags, and recent company milestones.

### 4. ✨ AI Cold Outreach Personalization Studio
- Generates context-aware, hyper-personalized cold outreach emails and LinkedIn InMails referencing the prospect's actual company news, tech stack, and intent triggers.
- **4 Communication Tones**:
  - `Value-First & ROI`: Consultative, metric-driven framing.
  - `Direct & Concise`: Crisp 3-sentence executive problem-solver.
  - `Casual Peer-to-Peer`: Warm and conversational.
  - `Executive Briefing`: Boardroom-level impact and CAC reduction.
- One-click copy to clipboard and native `mailto:` client trigger.

### 5. Kanban Pipeline & Stage Progression
- Visual sales progression across 4 stages: `Discovered`, `Outreach Sent`, `Meeting Booked`, and `Closed / Converted`.
- Quickly update stage directly with immediate state persistence.

### 6. Market Intelligence & Analytics Dashboard
- Aggregated KPI cards: Total Active Prospects, High-Intent Leads, Average Deliverability, and Average Match Score.
- Real-time proportional bar charts for Industry Distribution and Buying Intent Breakdown.

### 7. Custom Prospect Injection Modal
- Add your own leads with custom parameters and automatically run them through the simulated enrichment pipeline.
